Understand address screening and blocked transactions on the Relay platform
Copy page Copy page
The Relay platform screens transaction addresses using third-party risk data and an internal blocklist. Screening can prevent a quote or fill from proceeding.
​
How It Works
Screening uses:
Third-party data — Risk and sanctions databases, including HackBounty and Chainalysis, identify flagged addresses.
Internal blocklist — The platform maintains additional address restrictions based on threat reports and investigations.
​
Blocked Transactions
A flagged address can produce a “Detected sanctioned address” notification or a blocked-wallet error. See Unsupported Exchanges before sending from an exchange.
A fill-time screening failure does not trigger an automatic refund. See Execution Errors and contact Support for review.
