The Valinor BDC Exposure Fund (VBDC) is designed to deliver institutional private credit return with daily redemptions through a diversified basket of publicly traded Business Development Company (BDC) equities, tokenized as an onchain fund. Shares are available as onchain VBDC tokens or book-entry shares.
Complete the Investment Agreement (AML/KYC + accredited-investor and qualified-purchaser verification), fund via USD wire or USDC, and receive your VBDC tokens or book-entry shares at the next Market Day's NAV.
The Valinor BDC Exposure Fund (\"VBDC\" or the \"Fund\") is limited to investors that meet certain eligibility criteria. This website does not constitute an offer to sell any security. Any offer or sale of Shares in the Fund will be made only when a qualified offeree receives the Fund's Confidential Private Placement Memorandum (the \"PPM\") and definitive subscription documents, which contain material information not included here and should be read carefully in their entirety. Shares will be offered solely to persons who are accredited investors and are also qualified purchasers or knowledgeable employees. This website is qualified in its entirety by the PPM; if anything here conflicts with the PPM, the PPM controls.
Management fee. The Fund pays Valinor Digital Capital LLC (the \"Investment Manager\") a management fee at an annual rate of 1.25% of the Fund's average daily NAV, accrued daily and deducted monthly in arrears. The management fee is not the total cost of an investment — see \"Layered fees\" below.
Redemption / USDC. Shareholders may request redemptions as of each day that both the New York Stock Exchange and the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia are open (each such day, a \"Market Day\"). The Fund intends to meet redemptions from a liquidity buffer and from sales of portfolio holdings. Requests received by 2:00 p.m. ET on a Market Day will generally be processed at that day's NAV; the Fund expects to settle on or about the following Market Day. Redemptions are calculated and payable in USD and are subject to a redemption gate (currently up to 7.5% of NAV per Market Day), pro-ration, deferral, limitation, or suspension as described in the PPM. Shareholders may optionally elect to receive proceeds in USDC through Circle, a third party (the \"Payment Stablecoin Services\"), subject to Circle's separate terms — see \"Stablecoin and USDC services\" below.
Portfolio holdings. Portfolio holdings are presented on an aggregated and anonymized basis as of the date indicated and are unaudited. Positions are identified by generic labels rather than by name and may not refer to the same holding across periods. Holdings are determined in the Investment Manager's discretion, will change, and are not indicative of future composition. Nothing shown is, or constitutes, a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.
Underlying BDC risk. The Fund invests in the publicly traded equity of business development companies (\"BDCs\") and does not itself originate loans or have obligors. Through its BDC holdings, the Fund is indirectly exposed to the credit and default risk of underlying middle-market loans; BDC-level leverage; premium/discount-to-NAV volatility; payment-in-kind and other non-cash income; valuation risk; single-issuer and common-adviser concentration; smart-beta methodology and tracking-error risk; rebalancing and transaction costs; and BDC-level regulatory risk (1940 Act / RIC qualification). The Fund does not itself use leverage, though its net position may be leveraged when the leverage of its BDC holdings is aggregated.
Interest-rate and market risk. BDC share prices and the Fund's NAV are sensitive to interest rates, credit spreads, and general market conditions, and may decline materially over short periods.
Inter-series liability. As a series of a Delaware statutory trust, inter-series liability limitations are statutory but untested in court and bankruptcy.
Conflicts of interest. The Investment Manager and its affiliates are subject to actual and potential conflicts described in the PPM.
Reliance on key personnel. The Fund depends on the continued services of key personnel of the Investment Manager.
Operational and cyber risk. The Fund and its service providers are subject to operational errors, system failures, and cyber incidents.
